# 🌱 GreenDrop - Zero-Waste Community & Instant Disaster Relief Platform

GreenDrop is a full-stack material donation and disaster relief platform connecting generous donors with verified NGOs, local upcycling artists, and e-waste recyclers for 100% landfill diversion.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **✨ Zero-Waste Material Donation**: Multi-photo item listings with category & condition tags.
* **🚨 Emergency Disaster Relief Mode**: High-priority crisis broadcasting for immediate emergency supplies.
* **📋 NGO Structured Demands Board**: NGOs post item requirements directly for donors to fulfill.
* **♻️ Zero-Waste Recycle / Upcycle Tier**: Worn-out items route to verified textile recyclers & e-waste hubs.
* **🛡️ Dynamic QR-Code Handover**: Secure 6-digit dynamic QR pass generated on donor phone for verified collection.
* **🗺️ Interactive Map & Driver Route Planner**: Multi-stop pickup route optimization for NGO drivers.
* **📊 Environmental Impact Dashboard**: Tangible metrics (CO₂ saved, water conserved, landfill items diverted) & Eco-Badges.
* **💬 Direct 1-on-1 Messaging**: Real-time auto-polling chat for pickup coordination.
* **🏆 NGO Impact & Achievements Showcase**: Public profile inspector, reviews, ratings, and achievement highlights.
